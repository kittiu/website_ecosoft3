## **2.3 Why Open Source**

**PAGE HEADLINE**

Open source isn't a compromise. It's a competitive advantage.

*📝 This page directly addresses the most common objection: 'Is open source reliable enough for enterprise?'*

### **The Myth: Open Source Means Risky**

Many decision-makers assume that paid, proprietary software is inherently safer or more reliable than open source. This assumption is worth examining. SAP runs on Linux. Netflix runs on open source. The Thai government's most advanced science agency, NSTDA, replaced SAP with Ecosoft's Odoo implementation in 2018 — and has never looked back.

### **The Reality: Open Source Means Transparency**

With proprietary ERP, you are trusting a vendor's black box. With open source, every line of code is auditable, every module is inspectable, and every decision is documented publicly. Ecosoft delivers the full source code to every client at project completion. You own it outright. You can hand it to any competent developer in the world to maintain, extend, or migrate.

### **The Economics: No Licence \= More Budget for Excellence**

A typical SAP or Oracle implementation requires six- to seven-figure annual licence payments — before you pay a single consultant. Odoo Community Edition and ERPNext are 100% free. That budget stays in your organisation, where it can fund better implementation quality, more thorough training, and deeper system customisation.

### **Odoo CE \+ OCA vs Odoo Enterprise**

Ecosoft works exclusively with Odoo Community Edition, enhanced by OCA modules — not Odoo Enterprise. This means no per-user licence fees, access to hundreds of community-vetted modules, and the ability to use our own high-quality Thai-localisation modules that we have contributed back to the global OCA community.

Yes. Data migration is included in Standard and Enterprise packages. We migrate master data (customers, suppliers, products, chart of accounts) and can migrate historical transactions where technically feasible.

**What does post-go-live support look like?**

All packages include one year of support. After that, you choose: continue with an Ecosoft support retainer, maintain the system with your internal IT team, or hand the source code to any developer you choose. No lock-in.

### **About Cost**

**Why are there no licence fees?**

Because we implement 100% open-source software. Odoo Community Edition and ERPNext are free to use, modify, and distribute. You pay Ecosoft for expertise — consulting, implementation, customisation, and training — not for access to software.

**How does the total cost compare to SAP or Microsoft Dynamics?**

Significantly lower, typically by 60–80% over a 5-year horizon when licence costs are included. The implementation quality is comparable or better because our consultants are specialists in open-source platforms, not generalists spread across dozens of products.
